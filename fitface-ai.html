FitFace AI Face, Body, and Skin Coach

FitFace AI turns wellness and appearance-focused insights into structured actions. Adults can use face or body photos for AI-assisted analysis, then follow skincare guidance, adaptive workout plans, and daily progress tracking.

Face and skin analysis

  • Structured feedback for facial symmetry, jawline, eye area, cheekbones, and skin clarity
  • Morning and evening skincare routine guidance
  • Ingredient-focused coaching that can adapt to preferences and sensitivity
  • Comparison reports for tracking changes over time
  • Private on-device mode or advanced AI analysis

Body analysis and adaptive fitness plans

  • Recommendations for training focus, frequency, session length, and environment
  • Editable weekly workout plans with recovery and fallback sessions
  • Guided face and body workout sessions
  • Hydration, sleep, workout, step, and daily discipline tracking
  • Apple Health support when available

Youth-safe planning

Users under 18 receive a questionnaire-based fitness planning experience. Face and body photo analysis is not available to users under 18.

FitFace AI provides wellness, fitness, skincare, and appearance-focused coaching. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Privacy choices

Private on-device mode can process a face photo locally on the iPhone. Read the complete FitFace AI Privacy Policy for details about photo handling, derived measurements, retention, age restrictions, and third-party services.
