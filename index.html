FitMacro combines computer vision, verified food databases, and custom prompts to estimate nutrition with high precision. Users can track their meals, macros, and progress without spending minutes on every plate of food.

  • Instant nutrition from photos and text descriptions
  • Daily calorie and macro goals for cut, maintain, or gain
  • Multi-language interface and AI coaching
  • History of meals and progress over time

How it works under the hood

Our backend orchestrates image analysis, structured JSON prompts, and external nutrition data sources to generate consistent and transparent macro estimates. The system is designed for extensibility — from new food types to future integrations.

  • Layered AI prompts tailored for nutrition use-cases
  • Integration with high-quality food databases
  • Careful error handling and user-facing transparency
  • Architecture ready for future health-data integrations

Data protection & user trust

We design FitMacro with a simple idea: your data is about your health, not our ads. Our goal is to handle user information carefully and transparently, and to keep improving our protections as we grow.

  • We focus on using data to improve user experience and accuracy
  • No data reselling to third-party advertisers
  • Clear in-app messaging about how information is used
  • Planned future improvements to permissions and controls

For detailed information, please refer to the official privacy policy and terms once they are published on this site.
