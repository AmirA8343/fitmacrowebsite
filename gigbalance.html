GigBalance: Income, Mileage, and Expense Tracking

GigBalance gives rideshare drivers, delivery workers, freelancers, and other independent contractors one place to track the numbers behind their work. Record platform income, business mileage, deductible expenses, and receipts without maintaining separate spreadsheets.

Also see: Gig income tracker guide

Built for gig and 1099 work

  • Income records for Uber, Lyft, DoorDash, Uber Eats, Instacart, Skip, and other platforms
  • Base pay, tips, bonuses, trip count, online time, and active time
  • Screenshot import that helps fill earnings details faster
  • Manual and assisted business mileage records
  • Expense categories and attached receipt photos
  • Net earnings, hourly rates, mileage totals, and deduction-ready history

Prepare cleaner records for tax season

GigBalance keeps income, gas, parking, tolls, insurance, repairs, phone costs, supplies, mileage, and receipts together. Pro reporting tools include CSV exports and annual tax-ready reports for personal review or accountant handoff.
