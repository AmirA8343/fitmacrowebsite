GigBalance

GigBalance is a finance app from FitMacro Technologies Inc. that helps users track gig income and earnings from flexible work.

What GigBalance focuses on

  • Income tracking for gig and freelance work
  • Simple records for day-to-day earnings visibility
  • Practical workflow for independent workers
