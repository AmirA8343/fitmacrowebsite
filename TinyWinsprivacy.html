Privacy Policy for TinyWins

Effective Date: February 2026

TinyWins respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your information.

TinyWins does not collect, store, or transmit personal information.

All data you create in the app (habits, progress, streaks, and preferences) is stored locally on your device.

No account or login is required.

2. In-App Purchases

TinyWins offers optional subscriptions processed securely by Apple through the App Store.

We do not collect or store payment details. We only receive subscription status confirmation.

The app only uses Apple App Store services for purchase handling.

No advertising, tracking, or analytics services are used.

4. Data Security

Your data remains on your device and under your control.

Deleting the app removes all stored data.

5. Children’s Privacy

TinyWins does not knowingly collect information from children.

This policy may be updated as the app evolves.
