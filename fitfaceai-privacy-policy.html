Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 13, 2026

FitFace AI ("we," "our," or "the app") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when you use the app.

1. Face and Body Photo Data We Collect

FitFace AI collects face photos and body photos only when you choose to upload an image from your photo library or take a photo with your camera for analysis inside the app.

These photos may contain biometric or appearance-related information visible in the image, such as facial features, skin appearance, jawline appearance, posture, and body composition signals. FitFace AI does not collect face templates, faceprints, or identity-verification measurements.

2. How We Use Face and Body Photo Data

We use submitted face and body photos only to provide the features you request in the app, including:

  • Generating face analysis and body analysis results
  • Creating personalized workout, recovery, and improvement recommendations
  • Showing progress-related insights inside your account
  • Returning the requested analysis result to your device

We do not use submitted face or body photos for advertising, marketing profiling, or unrelated product analytics.

3. Who We Share Face and Body Photo Data With

When you submit a face or body photo for analysis, the image is temporarily processed through the following service providers:

  • Firebase Storage, which temporarily stores the uploaded image so the analysis request can be completed
  • Our backend service, which securely submits the analysis request
  • OpenAI, which processes the analysis request as our downstream AI service provider

We share face and body photo data with these providers only to complete the analysis you requested in the app.

4. How Long Face and Body Photo Data Is Retained

FitFace AI does not retain uploaded face or body photos after analysis is completed.

Uploaded face and body photos are stored temporarily in Firebase Storage only for the time needed to complete the analysis request, and are then deleted immediately after the request finishes.

We use this short temporary storage period only because the analysis workflow requires a transient file upload before the result can be returned to the app. We do not store uploaded face or body photos indefinitely.

FitFace AI may retain derived analysis results, workout recommendations, and account-related health tracking records in your account after the uploaded photo itself has been deleted.

5. Apple Health / HealthKit Data

If you choose to connect Apple Health / HealthKit, FitFace AI reads only the data needed for the Daily Tracking feature in this build, which includes steps, exercise minutes, and sleep data.

Apple Health / HealthKit data is used only to populate your in-app daily tracking, recovery summaries, and related health insights. HealthKit access is optional and can be declined or revoked in Apple Health settings at any time.

6. AI-Generated Exercise Disclaimer

Some exercise recommendations, demonstrations, and guidance within the app are generated with the assistance of artificial intelligence.

While we aim to provide helpful and accurate content, some exercises or instructions may occasionally contain mismatches, errors, or incorrect guidance.

Users should perform exercises carefully, use their own judgment, and stop immediately if an exercise feels unsafe or inappropriate.

If you notice incorrect exercises or guidance, you can report them through the Settings section of the app.

7. Third-Party Services

The app may use trusted third-party services to operate, including:

  • Firebase (Google) for authentication, storage, and backend infrastructure
  • OpenAI for requested AI analysis processing

These services follow their own privacy and security practices.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect user data and maintain secure communication between the app and our services.

9. Children's Privacy

The app is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any updates will be reflected by revising the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you may contact us through the app support page.
