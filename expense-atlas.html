Expense Atlas Business Expense Tracker

Expense Atlas is designed for freelancers, gig workers, entrepreneurs, and small-business owners who need a straightforward record of business income and expenses. New users can explore the workflow with 10 free logs.

Keep day-to-day business records organized

  • Record income and expenses by date
  • Add categories and notes to each entry
  • Track mileage and keep receipt records
  • Review financial history in one place
  • Use a secure account to maintain business records

How Expense Atlas differs from GigBalance

Expense Atlas is a general business record tracker for freelancers and small businesses. GigBalance is more specialized for rideshare, delivery, and 1099 workers who want platform-level earnings, work-time metrics, mileage deductions, and tax-ready reports.
