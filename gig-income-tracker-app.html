Gig Income and Mileage Tracker App for iPhone and Android

Gross deposits do not show what gig work actually earns after mileage and expenses. A dedicated gig tracker should keep platform income, tips, work time, mileage, receipts, and possible tax deductions in one consistent record.

What gig workers should track

  • Gross income, base pay, tips, bonuses, and promotions by platform
  • Online time and active time for calculating hourly earnings
  • Business mileage with date and route-area details
  • Gas, tolls, parking, repairs, insurance, phone costs, and supplies
  • Receipt photos and records that can support tax preparation

How GigBalance simplifies those records

  • Import earnings details from screenshots
  • Compare total income, expenses, net earnings, and hourly rates
  • Export history to CSV and create annual tax-ready reports with Pro
