Best Macro Tracker App for iPhone: FitMacro

FitMacro is a macro tracker app designed for users who want faster and more accurate nutrition tracking. It supports calorie tracking and macro logging from photos and text input.

What FitMacro helps with

  • Daily calorie and macro tracking
  • Protein, carbs, and fat visibility
  • AI-powered meal analysis for faster logging

Who this is for

  • People cutting, maintaining, or lean bulking
  • Users who want to reduce manual food logging
  • Athletes and everyday users who need consistency
