FitMacro Free Macro Tracker App

FitMacro is a free macro tracker app for iPhone and Android that helps you log calories, protein, carbohydrates, and fat from meal photos, barcode scans, or quick text entries. It is built for weight loss, muscle gain, and simple daily nutrition tracking without a long manual food search.

Download for iPhone Download for Android

What to look for in a macro tracking app

  • Clear daily protein, carbohydrate, fat, and calorie targets
  • Fast logging methods for both home-cooked and packaged food
  • Progress views that make under- and over-target nutrients obvious
  • Meal planning that supports cutting, maintenance, or muscle gain

How FitMacro tracks macros

  • AI analysis for meal photos and text descriptions
  • Barcode scanning for packaged food
  • Personalized meal plans, grocery lists, and AI nutrition coaching
  • Apple Health activity data alongside nutrition progress
  • Support for 11 languages
