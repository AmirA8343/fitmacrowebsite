TinyWins Pro: ADHD Planner

TinyWins helps people with ADHD, busy schedules, or inconsistent motivation turn large goals into small daily actions. It combines daily planning with focused work sessions and recovery tools for difficult days.

Also see: ADHD daily planner guide

Planning tools for focus and consistency

  • Daily Wins that break larger goals into manageable actions
  • Smart planning and time-blocked focus sessions
  • Focus timer for distraction-free work
  • Rescue Mode with smaller tasks for low-energy or overwhelmed days
  • End-of-day reflection, streaks, weekly wins, and progress insights

Who may find TinyWins useful

TinyWins is designed for ADHD minds and people who struggle with overwhelm, executive function, motivation, or maintaining a complicated productivity system. It can also be used as a straightforward daily planner by anyone who prefers small, concrete actions.
