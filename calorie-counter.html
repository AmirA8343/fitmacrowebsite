Frequently Asked Questions

What is a calorie counter?

A calorie counter is a tool that helps track daily calorie intake by logging meals and estimating nutritional values.

Is FitMacro a calorie tracker app?

Yes. FitMacro is a calorie tracker and macro tracking app designed for accurate, fast, and simple nutrition logging.

Can I scan food from a photo?

Yes. FitMacro uses AI-powered photo analysis to estimate food portions, calories, and macros. You can review the result before adding it to your log.

Does FitMacro include a meal planner?

Yes. FitMacro can generate personalized meal plans and grocery lists based on your nutrition goals and preferences.
