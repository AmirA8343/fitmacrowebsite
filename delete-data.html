User Data Deletion

Last updated: May 12, 2026

To request deletion of your FitMacro account data or app data, email fitmacro14@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account.

What to Include

  • Your account email address.
  • The app name: FitMacro or FitMacros.
  • A short note saying that you want your data deleted.

Processing

We will review your request, verify account ownership when needed, and delete eligible account data according to our privacy policy and legal obligations.
