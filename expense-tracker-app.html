Business Expense Tracker for Freelancers and Small Businesses

Freelancers and small-business owners need records that separate business activity from everyday spending. A useful expense tracker should make it fast to record income, categorize expenses, attach context, and review financial history.

Records worth keeping

  • Business income and client payments
  • Supplies, software, travel, mileage, and other work expenses
  • Receipts, notes, categories, and transaction dates
  • A consistent history for budgeting or tax preparation

Why this app is practical

  • Ten free logs let new users test the workflow
  • Fast manual entry keeps records understandable and editable
  • General business focus suits freelancers and small-business owners
